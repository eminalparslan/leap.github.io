LEAP — Leaders Emerge and Partner — is a regional community of emerging leaders ages 18 to 35 from diverse backgrounds, here to network, grow, and serve. Northern Virginia, founding 2026.

Mission

Our mission is to offer a platform where emerging leaders from diverse backgrounds come together — building a regional network that opens doors, fosters growth, and gives back.

Vision

We envision a connected generation of emerging leaders whose friendships across backgrounds strengthen our region.

What we hold to

    Nine committees, one community

    Everything LEAP does happens through a committee. Each one runs at least three projects a year, chosen by its own members. Press a tile to see what the work looks like.
