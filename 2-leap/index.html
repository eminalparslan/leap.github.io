LEP

Leaders Emerge and Partner

Network. Grow. Serve.

A regional community of emerging leaders, ages 18 to 35, from every background. Northern Virginia, founding 2026.

Mission

Our mission is to offer a platform where emerging leaders from diverse backgrounds come together — building a regional network that opens doors, fosters growth, and gives back.

Vision

We envision a connected generation of emerging leaders whose friendships across backgrounds strengthen our region.

What we hold to

Nine committees

Everything LEAP does happens through a committee. Each one runs at least three projects a year, chosen by its own members. Pick one to see what the work looks like.

Why people join

LEAP is being founded now by young adults and organizations across the region. If you want to be part of a committee — or start one — write to us.
