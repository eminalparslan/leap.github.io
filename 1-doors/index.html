LEP

Leaders Emerge and Partner

A regional community of emerging leaders, ages 18 to 35, from every background — here to network, grow, and serve.

Northern Virginia. Founding in 2026.

Mission

Our mission is to offer a platform where emerging leaders from diverse backgrounds come together — building a regional network that opens doors, fosters growth, and gives back.

Vision

We envision a connected generation of emerging leaders whose friendships across backgrounds strengthen our region.

Everything LEAP does happens through a committee. Each one runs at least three projects a year, chosen by its own members. Open a door to see what the work looks like.

Why people join
