LEAP is a regional community of emerging leaders ages 18 to 35 from diverse backgrounds — here to network, grow, and serve. Northern Virginia, founding 2026.

See the committees

Mission

Our mission is to offer a platform where emerging leaders from diverse backgrounds come together — building a regional network that opens doors, fosters growth, and gives back.

Vision

We envision a connected generation of emerging leaders whose friendships across backgrounds strengthen our region.

What we hold to

Nine committees across one region

Everything LEAP does happens through a committee. Each one runs at least three projects a year, chosen by its own members. Select a point on the map — or a name in the list — to see what the work looks like.

    Why people join

    LEAP is being founded now by young adults and organizations across the region. If you want to be part of a committee — or start one — write to us.
